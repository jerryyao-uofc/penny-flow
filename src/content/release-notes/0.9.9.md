## Recording and quick paste

Speech that the voice detector drops is still transcribed, as long as the microphone actually heard something. Short takes are no longer padded with silence, which was making the speech model return an empty transcript.

Quick paste inserts one of three saved strings exactly, without the extra space or Enter that a transcript paste can add. Set the text and the shortcut under General.

Penny Flow does not install upstream Handy releases. Update checks stay off.
